At FLoC/<a href="https://csf2026.ieee-security.org/">CSF 2026</a> in Lisbon, Portugal, our 2012 paper <a href="https://people.cispa.io/cas.cremers/downloads/papers/dh_tamarin_extended_v1.pdf">"Automated Analysis of Diffie-Hellman Protocols and Advanced Security Properties"</a>, by Benedikt Schmidt, Simon Meier, Cas Cremers, and David Basin, was awarded the IEEE CSF 2026 Test-of-Time award.
